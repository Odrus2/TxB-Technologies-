Questions about vehicle tracking?

Start with these answers. If your situation depends on a particular vehicle or device, contact TxB for a compatibility check.

What is GPS vehicle tracking?
A tracker uses satellite positioning and a communications network to report location and selected events to a platform. Reporting interval, accuracy and available vehicle data depend on the device, network and installation.
Does the website’s map show my real vehicles?
No. The animated map on the homepage is a demonstration route centered around Kampala. It does not connect to TxB’s tracking platform or represent a customer vehicle.
Can TxB track cars, motorcycles, trucks and equipment?
Different tracker types can be used for cars, motorcycles, commercial fleets, trailers and equipment. The right option depends on power access, environmental conditions, network coverage and the specific asset.
Can a tracker show fuel use or engine information?
Some devices can read supported CAN data or connect to compatible fuel and other sensors. Not every vehicle exposes the same information. TxB should verify the vehicle make, model, year and required parameters before quoting.
Will the tracker work in my country and on my mobile network?
Device variants support different cellular bands and technologies. Ask TxB to confirm the exact model variant and local network compatibility for your operating area before ordering.
Do you provide installation?
Installation can be discussed as part of a quote. Availability, location, vehicle access and installation scope need to be confirmed with TxB.
How much does a device and subscription cost?
Pricing depends on the device, installation, connectivity, platform functions, number of vehicles and support requirements. Send a quote request with those details for a tailored response.
Can I buy a tracker without installation?
Ask TxB about device-only supply and configuration options. Some installations require specialist vehicle knowledge and can affect warranty or safety systems if done incorrectly.
How does the quote form work?
This website is static. The form prepares an email in your email application; review it and send it to submit your inquiry. It does not automatically store or send the form from the website.
What information should I include in a quote request?
Include the number and type of vehicles or assets, where they operate, what you want to monitor, and whether you need installation. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal travel details.

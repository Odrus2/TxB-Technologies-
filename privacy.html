Privacy information

Website privacy notice

This draft explains what happens when you browse this static website or prepare a quote request.

Business owner review required. Confirm this notice reflects TxB’s actual practices and applicable local requirements before publishing.

Quote requests

The quote form runs in your browser and prepares an email addressed to the configured TxB inbox. The website itself does not transmit or store the form contents. Information is only sent if you choose Send in your email application. TxB should explain its retention and handling practices in its response or final published policy.

External services

The homepage loads map tiles from Esri and uses third-party hosted map and icon libraries. Those services may receive technical request information such as your IP address when the page or map loads; review their current privacy terms. The site does not include advertising pixels or analytics code in this package.

Vehicle location data

This marketing website does not display customer vehicle locations. The homepage map is an illustrative demo. Any future connected tracking platform should have separate access controls, security and a clear privacy notice for location data and affected drivers.

Contact

For a privacy inquiry, contact TxB at info@txbtechnologies.com, after confirming this is the correct published address.

Draft prepared 15 October 2026. Update the date and content when adopting a final privacy policy.

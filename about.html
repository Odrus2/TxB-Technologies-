About TxB Technologies

Technology should make work clearer.

We help customers explore vehicle tracking and telematics that can fit their vehicles, operations and goals.

Our approach

Start with the real question behind the tracking.

What needs to be visible? Which people need access? What should happen when a vehicle or asset reaches a location, stops unexpectedly or reports an event?

TxB’s role is to help identify suitable hardware and platform options, explain what the data can support, and plan a practical way to get started.

  • Clear scoping before product recommendations
  • Compatibility checks for vehicles, sensors and networks
  • Support discussions around installation and ongoing use

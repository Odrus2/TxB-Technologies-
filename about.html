About TxB

Technology that gives people a clearer view of every journey.

TxB Technologies Limited is building practical tracking and telematics solutions for vehicle owners and businesses.

Our approach

Start with the real operational need.

Tracking is most useful when device, connectivity and platform work together. TxB focuses on understanding what a customer needs to monitor, then identifying a suitable setup and support path.

Our aim is to make vehicle and asset information easier to access, understand and act on.

  • Solutions scoped to each vehicle, asset and operating environment
  • Clear explanation of device capabilities and limitations
  • Installation and support planned around the agreed solution

Proposed company motto

Smart mobility. Better control.

Proposed vision

To help make every journey and mobile operation safer, smarter and more visible.

Proposed mission

To make dependable tracking technology practical through suitable devices, clear information and responsive support.

What guides us

Principles for a useful service

Reliability

Practicality

Service

Make setup and ongoing support straightforward for customers.

Responsibility

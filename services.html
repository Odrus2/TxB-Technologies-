Service capabilities

Tracking services designed around your operation.

From a single vehicle to a working fleet, scope the tracking, alerts, hardware and support that fit your vehicles and operating conditions.

Every setup is scoped first. Availability depends on vehicle compatibility, local mobile network coverage, device options and the platform features agreed with TxB.
Location

Vehicle GPS tracking

View current reported location, trip history, stops and movement events through a connected tracking platform.

Useful for: private vehicles, company cars, motorcycles and commercial vehicles.
Operations

Fleet management

Bring vehicle activity together to support dispatch visibility, fleet utilization and operational follow-up.

Scope can include fleet groups, user access, reports and alerts.
Route control

Trip history & geofences

Review journeys and define virtual zones to help identify arrivals, departures or activity outside expected areas.

Geofence and trip functions depend on the platform selected.
Safety

Driver behavior & safety

Where supported, use configurable events for speeding, harsh driving, excessive idling, towing or crash-related alerts.

Data availability varies by device, installation and vehicle.
Vehicle data

CAN & vehicle telematics

For compatible models, read selected vehicle parameters such as mileage, fuel, engine data or status from supported interfaces.

Vehicle make, model, year and interface compatibility must be checked first.
Efficiency

Fuel monitoring

Explore sensor, CAN or fuel-level integrations to improve visibility into fuel use and unusual changes.

Requires compatible sensors/interfaces and calibration; results depend on installation.
Cold chain

Temperature & sensor monitoring

Connect compatible temperature, door, humidity or BLE sensors for selected cargo and asset monitoring use cases.

Sensor, range and alert support are confirmed during specification.
Assets

Asset & equipment tracking

Consider wired or battery-backed solutions for trailers, machinery and mobile assets that need location visibility.

Device choice depends on power access, environment and expected reporting frequency.
Deployment

Installation & technical support

Discuss device selection, installation planning, configuration and support for the agreed tracking solution.

Installation location and service availability are arranged by quotation.
